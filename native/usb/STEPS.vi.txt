VIETNAV native HUD BLE - ban thu 0.1.0

Muc tieu lan nay: kiem controller, de VietMap tim thay VIETNAV HUD,
bat tay va luu du lieu dan duong. CHUA hien huong re len HUD Mazda.
Chua thu voi radio Mazda/iPhone that; TPMS tam nghi trong lan thu HUD.

Chuan bi
- USB FAT32 RIENG, trong. Giai nen noi dung ZIP vao goc USB.
  Khong gop vao USB TPMS/CarPlay/Vietnav 0.9.1 dang dung.
- Can TPMS v3 native da cai voi wrapper dung ban duoc ho tro va BLE pack.
  Installer tu kiem tra; thieu hoac khac ban thi tu choi.
- Dung xe, bat ACC, doi man hinh san sang. Chi cam mot USB TouchTune.
  Boot trigger dung co che TouchTune cua cac kit truoc; firmware chua
  duoc kiem thu tren xe khong duoc dam bao tuong thich.

1. Cam USB, cho menu VIETNAV HUD - THU BLE.
   Chon KIEM TRA > DAT LICH. Rut USB sau thong bao thanh cong.
2. Tat xe, mo cua, cho man hinh tat han, doi them 30 giay, roi mo xe.
   Doi 2 phut de BLE khoi tao va ghi ket qua kiem tra.
3. Tat/mo xe nhu tren lan nua de tro ve TPMS. Cam USB.
   Chon KHAC > LUU LOG de luu ket qua vao native-hud-logs.
   Chi khi query thanh cong moi cho phep chon KET NOI.
4. Chon KET NOI > DAT LICH. Rut USB, tat/mo xe nhu buoc 2.
   Sau 60-90 giay, vao muc ket noi HUD trong VietMap Live tren iPhone,
   tim VIETNAV HUD, ket noi, bat dau mot lo trinh.
   Cua so thu toi da 180 giay tinh sau khoi tao BLE; ngat ket noi thi dung.
5. Cam lai USB, chon KHAC > LUU LOG. Gui thu muc native-hud-logs
   va native-touch.log de xac nhan bat tay/navigation/camera thuc te.
   Lan khoi dong tiep theo tu quay ve TPMS.

GO BO: KHAC > GO BO, xac nhan, rut USB va tat/mo xe.
HUY LICH: KHAC > HUY LICH huy lan thu chua chay. Khong dung ngay
tien trinh dang chay; tat/mo xe de quay lai TPMS.

Ban thu khong ghi de libtpms-v3.so, bds.real, BLE pack hay config TPMS.
Backup wrapper cu: /jci/bds/bds.vietnav-prev.
Log/transaction: /data_persist/vietnav-native.
Go ban thu nay truoc khi cap nhat/go TPMS. Neu wrapper bi tool khac sua,
go bo tu choi ghi de; giu nguyen backup va gui log de doi chieu.
Neu bao RESTORE_INCOMPLETE, khong xoa backup/USB, gui log de khoi phuc.

Khong tu reboot/kill bds tu menu. Watchdog chi dung PID cua lan thu
da xac minh neu mat nhip/log; lan start tiep theo dung wrapper TPMS.
Khong bao dam TPMS/wireless CarPlay/phone hoat dong dong thoi khi thu HUD.
Chua nop ban thu nay vao bo cai on dinh 0.9.1.
