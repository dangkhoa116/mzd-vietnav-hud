LAY LOG CMU / TPMS / SPEEDMAP
============================
1. Do xe, de dau Mazda hoat dong. Cam USB.
2. Hop thoai cap nhat Mazda -> cai dat.
3. Man hinh dau tien: CAI DAT / Log / HUY -> bam Log.
4. Doi "Da luu log". Bam OK, rut USB va mang ve may.

Thu muc cmu-logs-* gom:
- CPU/RAM va tien trinh truoc/sau, kernel va bao cao reset cua Mazda.
- Thong bao CPU/RAM/VIP va loi he thong tu nhat ky dang co.
- TPMS: log giao dien, Bluetooth/daemon, RAM, lan chay truoc va cai dat.
- SpeedMap: drive/hmi/boot/update, health.log va ban cu, trang thai.
- Log cai dat/cham cua CarPlay va VIETNAV neu co.

HUY chi thoat, khong thu log. Log khong cai dat hay khoi dong lai.
Khong bat them ghi log nen; chi doc bang chung hien co. File thieu la
binh thuong. Nhat ky cu co the thuoc lan khoi dong truoc.
Van ban duoc an thong tin rieng tu va gioi han dung luong; manifest.txt
ghi tep nguon va pham vi. Khong chep UART, core dump hay du lieu ban do.
Neu bao "chua xong / Collection incomplete", mang USB ve de kiem tra
cmu-logs-menu.log.

ENGLISH
=======
Park with Mazda Connect running. Insert USB -> Mazda update: install -> Log.
Wait for the completion message, tap OK, then remove the USB.
cmu-logs-* contains CMU, TPMS and SpeedMap diagnostics plus available kit
install/touch logs. HUY only exits. Log does not install or restart anything.
Only existing evidence is read; no continuous logger is enabled. Files may
be missing or belong to earlier boots. Text is privacy-filtered and bounded;
see manifest.txt. No UART captures, raw core dumps or map databases copied.
If collection fails, bring the USB home with cmu-logs-menu.log.
