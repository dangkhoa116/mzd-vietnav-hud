VIETNAV HUD - cac buoc tren xe (cai qua USB TouchTune)
========================================================

CAN CHUAN BI
  * Dia USB FAT32 duoc tao boi:  python installer/build-kit.py <o-dia>
    Kit mang san CA HAI mod, cai la chay:
      AA      oem-aa-mod 0.10.0 (Android Auto -> HUD, GAL 1.6)
      CARPLAY bridge mazda-carplay-hud (ban ilshyma: fix km/h + fold tieng Viet)
    Nguon goc + md5 tung file: vietnav-payload/PAYLOAD-SOURCES.md

CAI DAT (bam cam ung, khong can ban phim)
  1. Bat dien (ACC / may chay), cho radio khoi dong day du.
  2. Cam dia USB. May hoi cap nhat sau vai giay.
  3. Bam CAI DAT. Menu VIETNAV HUD hien len (trang thai o tren, song ngu
     Viet / Anh):
       CAI DAT   -> chon: AA / CARPLAY / CA HAI (ca hai), roi bam CAI DAT
                    lan nua de xac nhan (HUY = khong doi gi)
       THU THAP  -> ghi vietnav-collect-<ngay>.tgz ra dia USB
       GO BO     -> (khi da cai) bam GO BO lan nua de xac nhan, roi go
                    phan da cai
  4. Sau CAI DAT:
       AA:      cam dien thoai Android, mo dan duong
       CARPLAY: ket noi iPhone, mo dan duong (Apple Maps hoac
                VIETMAP LIVE)
     HUD se hien mui ten + khoang cach; ten duong tu dong fold dau
     tieng Viet cho vua font HUD.

NEU KHONG THAY HOP THOAI CAP NHAT
  * Rut dia, doi 10 giay, cam lai. Kiem tra dia FAT32 va o goc co
    jci-autoupdate + file .up.
  * Du phong bang terminal (root shell; dia la /tmp/mnt/sda1 khi ban phim
    cam cong kia). Khong co --yes thi chi chay thu, khong doi gi:
      sh /tmp/mnt/sda1/vietnav-install.sh all           (kiem tra, khong doi gi)
      sh /tmp/mnt/sda1/vietnav-install.sh aa --yes      (chi Android Auto)
      sh /tmp/mnt/sda1/vietnav-install.sh cp --yes      (chi CarPlay)
      sh /tmp/mnt/sda1/vietnav-install.sh all --yes     (ca hai)

THU THAP (chi khi co loi)
  * Khong can lam truoc khi cai: installer tu kiem tra may va DUNG truoc
    khi doi gi neu thay dieu bat thuong.
  * Neu CAI DAT bao loi hoac HUD khong hien, bam THU THAP roi gui file
    .tgz (firmware + sm.conf + XML AAP) kem cac file vietnav-*.log tren
    dia USB.

GO BO
  * Cung menu, bam GO BO. Neu mot dich vu khong khoi dong lai duoc thi
    tat dien khoi dong lai la xong.

LOG
  * Moi lan chay deu de log tren dia USB: vietnav-touch.log,
    vietnav-install.log, vietnav-rollback.log. Mang dia ve nha.

MOT DIA, NHIEU KIT
  * Dia co the mang ca kit TPMS va kit CarPlay khong day. Bam INSTALL se
    hoi chon kit - man chon luon hien 3 nut chuan (TPMS / CARPLAY /
    VIETNAV) moi khi dia co it nhat 2 trong 3 kit, moi kit co mot dong
    bao da cai tren xe hay chua (DA CAI / CHUA CAI); kit nao khong co tren
    dia van con nut, bam vao se bao ngay tren man hinh roi quay lai man
    chon. Nut huy la HUY trong menu cua tung kit (kit CarPlay hoi
    BAT DAU / HUY truoc). Sau TPMS hoac VIETNAV, bam dong thong bao
    cuoi roi LAM TIEP / XONG hoi co chon kit nua khong; kit CarPlay tu
    khoi dong lai may nen phai chay sau cung. Moi kit chi dung vao file
    cua no.
  * Sau khi GO BO (rollback) kit CarPlay khong day, menu nay co the bao
    "CHUA CAI" lai: bam CAI DAT de dua dong HUD tro lai.

LUU Y
  * Neu block jciCARPLAY/jciAAPA/aap_service da co environ_var cua mod khac:
    installer DUNG truoc khi doi bat cu gi va ghi ro trong log - xu ly mod
    kia truoc.
  * File payload sai md5 (USB hong) cung lam installer DUNG truoc khi doi
    gi: xay lai USB. Neu dang ghi ma loi giua chung, installer tra lai
    moi thu no da doi.
  * Ben CarPlay, qua trinh cai dat dat NaviSupported=TRUE (de iPhone gui
    du lieu dan duong iAP2); go bo chi tra ve FALSE neu truoc do la FALSE.
