VIETNAV HUD - cac buoc tren xe (cai qua USB TouchTune)
========================================================

CAN CHUAN BI
  * Dia USB FAT32 duoc tao boi:  python installer/build-kit.py <o-dia>
    Kit mang san CA HAI mod, cai la chay:
      AA      oem-aa-mod 0.10.0 (Android Auto -> HUD, GAL 1.6)
      CARPLAY bridge mazda-carplay-hud (ban ilshyma: fix km/h + fold tieng Viet)
    Nguon goc + md5 tung file: vietnav-payload/PAYLOAD-SOURCES.md

CAI DAT (bam cam ung, khong can ban phim)
  1. Bat dien (ACC / may chay), cho radio khoi dong day du.
  2. Cam dia USB. May hoi cap nhat sau vai giay.
  3. Bam CAI DAT. Menu VIETNAV HUD hien len:
       CAI DAT   -> chon: AA / CARPLAY / CA HAI (ca hai)
       THU THAP  -> ghi vietnav-collect-<ngay>.tgz ra dia USB
       GO BO     -> (khi da cai) go bo phan da cai
  4. Sau CAI DAT:
       AA:      cam dien thoai Android, mo dan duong
       CARPLAY: ket noi iPhone, mo dan duong (Apple Maps hoac
                VIETMAP LIVE)
     HUD se hien mui ten + khoang cach; ten duong tu dong fold dau
     tieng Viet cho vua font HUD.

NEU KHONG THAY HOP THOAI CAP NHAT
  * Rut dia, doi 10 giay, cam lai. Kiem tra dia FAT32 va o goc co
    jci-autoupdate + file .up.
  * Du phong bang terminal (root shell):
      sh /mnt/sdb1/vietnav-install.sh aa     (chi Android Auto)
      sh /mnt/sdb1/vietnav-install.sh cp     (chi CarPlay)
      sh /mnt/sdb1/vietnav-install.sh all    (ca hai)

BUOI THU THAP (nen lam ngay lan dau)
  * Bam THU THAP va giu file .tgz - co firmware + sm.conf + XML AAP,
    quyet dinh cac cau mo (ho tro 74.00.331, nguon ten duong).

GO BO
  * Cung menu, bam GO BO. Neu mot dich vu khong khoi dong lai duoc thi
    tat dien khoi dong lai la xong.

LUU Y
  * Neu block jciCARPLAY/jciAAPA da co LD_PRELOAD cua mod khac: installer
    se BO QUA dich vu do va ghi ro trong log - xu ly mod kia truoc.
  * Ben CarPlay, qua trinh cai dat dat NaviSupported=TRUE (de iPhone gui
    du lieu dan duong iAP2); go bo se tra ve FALSE.
